🌐 About Our Parkinson’s Detection System

Our Parkinson’s Detection System is an advanced AI-powered web application designed to assist in the early diagnosis and monitoring of Parkinson’s disease using multiple biometric inputs.

The goal is to provide accessible, non-invasive, and intelligent tools that help doctors and individuals identify signs of the disease at an early stage.

💡 Key Features

  • 🧠 Voice Detection: Uses signal processing and machine learning to analyze speech patterns and detect subtle vocal changes associated with Parkinson’s.
  • ✍️ Spiral Drawing Analysis: Evaluates smoothness and tremor intensity in hand-drawn spirals using AI-driven image recognition.
  • 🕺 Posture & Movement Detection: Uses deep learning-based video analysis to assess posture, gait, and movement abnormalities.
