Alzheimer’s Early Screening Suite

This module provides quick, non-invasive screening tools for early cognitive and brain changes: brain MRI scan, speech coherence, and word memory. It is meant for screening only, not a final diagnosis.

Brain MRI Scan (Alzheimer’s)

Upload a brain MRI slice. The AI model estimates the likelihood of Alzheimer’s-related atrophy patterns using a trained classifier.

Speech Coherence Test

Patient describes their morning routine. The system analyzes structure, repetition, and coherence, optionally with an AI model.

Word Memory Test

Immediate recall of a short word list, a classic early Alzheimer’s screening task.

Alzheimer’s Risk Summary

Combines MRI, speech, and memory scores into a single Alzheimer’s Early Risk Index (AERI).
