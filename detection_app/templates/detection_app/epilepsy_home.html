Epilepsy Screening Module

This is a screening questionnaire to estimate epilepsy risk based on symptoms, family history and triggers. It is not a final diagnosis, but helps decide whether a person should be referred to a specialist.

{% csrf_token %}

1. Has the person ever had episodes of sudden fainting, blackouts, or unexplained collapses?

2. During any episode, has anyone noticed jerking or stiffening of the arms, legs or whole body?

3. Is there a family history of epilepsy or repeated seizures?

4. Do these episodes often happen after lack of sleep, extreme stress, or missed meals?

5. After an episode, does the person feel confused, very tired, or unable to remember what happened?

6. Has there ever been tongue biting or loss of bladder control during an episode?

7. Does the person feel unwell (headache, eye strain, dizziness) when watching fast flashing lights or patterns?

8. Is the person currently taking anti-seizure (anti-epileptic) medicines prescribed by a doctor?
